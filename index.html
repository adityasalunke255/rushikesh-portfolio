I don't just create content — I build media properties.

🏆 Flagship Case Study

Chitale Bandhu

Full-stack social media strategy for Maharashtra's most iconic dairy brand. From objectives to phased execution to measurable outcomes — on a defined budget.

+

Objectives

  • Increase brand awareness among younger demographics
  • Drive engagement on Instagram & Facebook
  • Position Chitale Bandhu as modern while honoring heritage
  • Build content library for sustained posting

Target Audience

  • Tier 1 cities: Mumbai, Pune, Nashik
  • Tier 2: Nagpur, Aurangabad, Kolhapur
  • Age 18–35: new-gen Maharashtrians
  • NRI diaspora with nostalgia-driven connection

Phase 1: Engagement Boost

Weeks 1–2

  • Meme-format content linking heritage with pop culture
  • Festive moment marketing (tied to Marathi calendar)
  • Influencer seeding with food bloggers
  • Community polls and interactive stories

Phase 2: Sustained Hype

Weeks 3–4

  • User-generated content campaigns
  • Behind-the-scenes factory content
  • Cross-platform amplification
  • Performance analysis and optimization

Budget

  • Tier structure: ₹1.5L – ₹3L
  • Content creation: 40%
  • Paid promotion: 35%
  • Influencer collaboration: 25%
Influencer Marketing

Diljit Dosanjh × Mumbai Dabbawala

The cultural crossover that proved a meme page can drive real-world brand moments.

+

"Marathi Thecha with Punjabi Tadka"

When Diljit Dosanjh's Mumbai concert became the talk of the city, I spotted the cultural moment: Mumbai's dabbawala heritage meets Punjab's biggest star. The result was a content piece that crossed language barriers, got shared across fan communities, and proved that a meme page can create genuine cultural commentary — not just react to it.

Best-performing influencer-marketing content piece. Organic reach across multiple state audiences.

App Campaign

InDrive App

Influencer-led app install campaign targeting Mumbai's daily commuters with relatable, meme-native content.

+

Created a series of meme-format posts positioning InDrive as the commuter's secret weapon against surge pricing. Leveraged Mumbai's auto-rickshaw culture and local humor to drive app installs through organic shares rather than paid ads.
